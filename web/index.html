Connect the pi-top field station

  1. Put the laptop and the pi-top on the same WiFi or phone hotspot.
  2. Copy the sensor program to the pi-top: download well_sensor.py
  3. Plug the ultrasonic sensor into port D3 and fix it above the water, facing straight down.
  4. On the pi-top run:
    python3 well_sensor.py LAPTOP-ADDRESS
  5. When this bar shows the sensor live: fill the container and press Container is full now, pour it out and press Container is empty now, then refill.
